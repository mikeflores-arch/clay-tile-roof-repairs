# Real-Photo Shot List — Area Page Upgrades

The 30 area pages currently use AI-generated hero images (`public/images/areas/`).
Real photos beat them for E-E-A-T — replace one at a time as you get them.

## How to shoot
- Phone is fine. Landscape orientation, mid-morning or golden hour.
- From the **public sidewalk/street only** — no driveways, no drones over property.
- Get the **tile roof prominent** in frame; whole streetscapes work great too.
- Avoid: people, license plates, house numbers straight-on.

## How to deploy a replacement
Drop the photo in `area-heroes/real/` named `<slug>.jpg`, then tell Claude —
or run: resize to 1600w webp at `public/images/areas/<slug>.webp`, rebuild, push.
(Any real tile *job* photo from an actual project beats a streetscape — use those first.)
